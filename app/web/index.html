            How it works
            click “Draw route”, then click waypoints on the map in walking order (drag them to adjust). Elevation is loaded from the best NASA/USGS model (HiRISE 1 m, CTX 20 m, else MOLA); every stretch is coloured by slope and checked for your profile. “Improve” keeps your waypoints as via points and replaces each piece with the safest path between them.

            How it works
            set A and B (optionally via points). The planner first finds a corridor on coarse elevation data, then plans legs of ≤ 1.8 km on a fine grid where every cell is checked against all DTM pixels inside it. It avoids slopes above your critical limit, steep grades, loose ground (THEMIS night IR) and areas without elevation data, and minimises travel time with your profile's physics. Unsafe spots found in the detailed check are fed back and the corridor is re-planned (up to 14 rounds). Priority shifts between fastest and safest.

            Who travels?

            Movement and slope limits

            Slope limits are computed from Mars gravity, mass, power, soil friction and geometry. Check the proposal and apply it (or set your own limits).

            Calculation

            Sliding and tip-over limits do not depend on mass or gravity (m·g cancels out). Mass and gravity decide through P = m·g·v·(sin θ + Crr·cos θ) how steep the vehicle can still climb with the given power.
